How NoticeNest works

Official notices are often long PDFs. NoticeNest turns each one into a short card with the dates that matter to you.

The steps

  1. It checks the notice pages you configured, politely and not too often, and skips notices it has already seen.
  2. It reads the notice text (PDF or web page).
  3. It finds the type, dates, fees and who the notice is for. With an AI key it uses an AI model; without one it uses patterns.
  4. A validator checks every date and fee against the notice text. Anything it cannot find is marked "needs review" instead of hidden.
  5. It matches the notice to your course and semester and sends reminders 3 days before, 1 day before and on the day.
  6. If a notice is edited or a revision is published, it shows what changed.

What it cannot promise

  • It can miss a date or misread a scan. Always open the original notice before you act.
  • If a notice does not say who it is for, NoticeNest shows it as "may apply to you" so you do not miss it.
  • Only public notice text is ever sent to an AI service. Your profile stays on this computer.
