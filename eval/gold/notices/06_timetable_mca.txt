Title: Time Table for MCA I Semester Examination
Date: 25.10.2026
---
DEMO UNIVERSITY - EXAMINATION BRANCH

The theory examinations of MCA I Semester will commence from 23.11.2026 and end on 05.12.2026.
Examination centres and the seating plan will be displayed three days before the first paper.
Admit cards can be downloaded from 17.11.2026.
