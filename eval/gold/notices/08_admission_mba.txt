Date: 20.10.2026

DEMO UNIVERSITY - ADMISSION CELL

Counselling for admission to the MBA programme (first semester) will be held in two rounds.
Round 1 counselling: 04.11.2026.
Round 2 counselling: 11.11.2026.
Candidates must pay the admission fee of Rs. 25,000 within three days of seat allotment.
