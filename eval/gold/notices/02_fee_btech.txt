Title: Payment of Semester Fee for B.Tech 2nd, 4th and 6th Semester (Even Semester)
Date: 25.10.2026
---
DEMO UNIVERSITY - ACCOUNTS SECTION

Students of B.Tech 2nd, 4th and 6th semester are required to deposit the semester fee through the online payment portal.

Fee without fine: Rs. 42,500 up to 02.11.2026.
A late fine of Rs. 1,000 will be charged if the fee is deposited between 03.11.2026 and 09.11.2026.
No payment will be accepted after 09.11.2026.
